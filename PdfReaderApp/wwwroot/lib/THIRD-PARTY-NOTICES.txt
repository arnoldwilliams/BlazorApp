Third-party libraries bundled with this application
==================================================

mespeak / meSpeak.js (v2.0.7, based on speak.js and eSpeak)
  Used for offline text-to-speech synthesis during audio export.
  Source: https://www.masswerk.at/mespeak
  Licence: GNU GPL (eSpeak and the code derived from speak.js are GPL).
  Files: mespeak.js, mespeak-core.js, voices/*

lamejs (v1.2.1, by Alex Zhukov, a JavaScript port of LAME)
  Used to encode synthesised audio as MP3.
  Source: https://github.com/zhuker/lamejs
  Licence: LGPL-3.0. LAME is used here unmodified, linked as a separate
  script (lame.min.js), as required by the LGPL.
  Files: ../lamejs/lame.min.js

pdf.js
  Used to render PDF documents and expose their text layer.
  Source: https://mozilla.github.io/pdf.js/
  Licence: Apache-2.0
  Files: ../pdfjs/*
